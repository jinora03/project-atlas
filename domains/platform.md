The platform domain is the foundation everything else runs on: how requests are
routed, how content is delivered, how software is deployed, and how you know it
is healthy once it's live.

It spans the outer edges of the [Systems Map](/systems-map) — the network path
in, and the infrastructure and observability tiers underneath.
